# Masque for Mac

The Masque app puts your Masque character into OBS for you: it finds OBS, switches on OBS's connection for apps,
and walks you through picking your camera with big buttons. It then sits in the menu bar so your character's mouth
moves with your voice and it laughs when you laugh.
